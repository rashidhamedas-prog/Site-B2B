# Frontend Design

Approach this as the design lead at a design studio known for giving every client a distinct visual identity. This client already rejected cliché or templated proposals.

## Ground designs in the subject matter

Taranom is a Mashhad womenswear workshop selling linen/cotton tunics and coats — retail singles on `.ir`, wholesale packs on `.com`. Visual choices come from fabric, workshop, and RTL Persian commerce — not generic SaaS cards.

## Design principles used in this pass

- One memorable motion: the news ticker. No scattered section reveals.
- Hero is cinematic and slim so catalog starts in the first viewport.
- CTAs name the next action, not a vague "submit".
- Keep existing Vazirmatn + forest/gold tokens. Do not introduce a second display face or a red "conversion" palette.
- Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`. Animate only `transform` / `opacity`.
- Persian copy stays H2H and fact-checked. No fake urgency.
